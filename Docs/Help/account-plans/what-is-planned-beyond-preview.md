# What is planned beyond Preview?

**Planned. Not available yet.** Everything on this page describes direction,
not something you can use or buy today. No dates, and no prices.

## The shape of the product

Onceaway is built as a sequence, and each stage only makes sense once the one
before it is trustworthy:

**Observe** → **Detect** → **Confirm** → **Understand** → **Assess** →
**Recommend** → **Assist**

Everything up to and including Recommend exists today. Assist exists in a
deliberately small form.

## What a paid tier would cover

The thinking is that the observing, detecting, confirming, understanding,
assessing and recommending part of the product is the core, and that is where a
paid tier would sit. **Planned.**

No price is set, and none is implied anywhere in the app.

## The Assist direction

Assist is the part that acts on your behalf, so it grows most slowly.

- **Today:** one action — moving a single file you chose into a folder you
  chose, after you approve the exact move.
- **Planned:** more approved assisted actions of the same kind, each one
  previewed and approved individually.
- **Later, and further off:** helping set up repeatable routines you have asked
  for.

Whatever gets added, the rule it is built on does not move: no observed event,
AI output or recommendation can directly cause an action. See
[Why Onceaway asks before it acts](../using-onceaway/why-onceaway-asks-before-it-acts.md).

## What is not planned

A productivity score, employee monitoring, or anything that reports on one
person to another. Those are not on the roadmap at all.
