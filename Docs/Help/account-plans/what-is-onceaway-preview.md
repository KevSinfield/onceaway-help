# What is Onceaway Preview?

Preview is what everyone is on today. It is the whole product as it currently
exists, with nothing held back and nothing to pay.

## What it means

- **No account.** There is no sign-up, no sign-in and no profile.
- **No billing.** Nothing is charged, no payment method is collected, and there
  is no payment system in the app at all.
- **No tiers.** Nobody is on a free plan being nudged toward a paid one,
  because neither exists yet.

The account menu in the top right shows *Onceaway Preview* and says the same
thing: plans have not launched, nothing is charged, and no account is needed.

## Is billing active?

No.

There is no billing backend, no card handling and no subscription. **Upgrade to
Pro**, **Invite your team** and **Get a free month** open short informational
screens describing ideas. They take no payment and create nothing.

## Why show them at all?

So the shape of the product is honest about where it is heading, and so those
ideas can be discussed with the people using it before anything is built. They
are labelled as previews rather than dressed up as working features.

## What about my data if plans launch?

Your activity history, reviews and preferences live on your Mac and are yours.
Nothing about them depends on an account, because there is no account.
