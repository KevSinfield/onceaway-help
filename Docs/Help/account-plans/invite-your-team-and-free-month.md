# Invite your team, and Get a free month

Both are **previews of ideas**. Neither is available yet, and neither does
anything today.

## Invite your team

**Not available yet.**

The screen describes the idea of using Onceaway alongside colleagues. There is
no invitation to send, no team to join, and no shared account — there is no
account system at all.

If it is ever built, it would have to work without undermining the things that
make Onceaway what it is. Your history stays on your Mac, and nothing reports
on one person to another. Anything shared would have to be shared deliberately,
by you.

## Get a free month

**Not available yet.**

The screen describes the idea of a referral. There is nothing to refer anyone
to, no month to give away, and no billing for a free month to apply to.

## Why they are visible

They set out where the product might go, so the people using it can push back
early. Opening either one takes no payment, creates no account, sends nothing
and signs you up to nothing.
