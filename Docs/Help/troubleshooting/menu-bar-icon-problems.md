# The menu-bar icon is missing or dashed

Two different situations that look similar at a glance.

## The ring is dashed

Nothing is wrong. **A dashed ring means observation is off.**

Click the ring and choose **Start Observation**. The ring becomes solid and the
menu says *Observation: On*.

Observation is off when you first install Onceaway, and after you pause it.
Quitting also stops it.

## The ring is not there at all

**You are not stuck.** Open Onceaway and go to **Home**: it shows the current
state and starts or pauses observation, exactly as the menu does. Nothing
depends on finding the ring.

Then, if you would like it back, work through these:

- **Is Onceaway running?** Check your Applications folder and open it. Closing
  the main window does not quit the app, but quitting it does remove the ring.
- **Is your menu bar full?** macOS hides icons when there is no room,
  especially on laptops with a notch. Try quitting another menu-bar app, or
  widening the space, and see whether it reappears.
- **Are other icons hidden by a menu-bar manager?** Tools like Bartender and
  similar can hide it. Check their settings for Onceaway.
- **Try quitting and reopening Onceaway.**

## Finding the ring

It is a small circle, near your clock. Solid while observing, dashed while not,
with a small dot in the gap at the top right. It follows your menu bar's
appearance, so it is dark on a light bar and light on a dark one.
