# What is Insights?

History is what happened. Insights is what Onceaway has learned about your
repeated work over time.

## The difference

**History** is the raw timeline: app activity and coarse file and browser
events, in order, kept for as long as your retention setting says.

**Insights** does not list events at all. It looks across your Patterns and
your answers and shows you shape and direction: how much you have noticed, how
much you confirmed, what recurs most, which days were busiest for repeated
work, and what is happening more often than it used to.

## What it shows

- **Counts** — Patterns noticed, confirmed, opportunities, and how many you
  said you would like reduced.
- **From noticed to worth reducing** — each stage as a smaller group of the one
  above it, so you can see where things drop out.
- **Repeated work by category** — grouped as websites, files, or moving between
  apps.
- **Top repeated activities** — ranked by how often they happened.
- **Days with repeated work** — how many different repeated activities appeared
  on each day.
- **What's increasing** — repeated work happening more often than in the
  previous period.

You can look at the last 7 or 30 days.

## What it will never show

No score. No rating. No "focus" figure, no time-wasted figure, and no judgement
about whether a day was well spent. Insights describes repeated work; it does
not grade you.
