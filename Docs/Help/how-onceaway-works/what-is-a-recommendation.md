# What is a Recommendation?

A Recommendation is an idea for reducing a piece of repeated work. It is
optional, AI-assisted, and it appears only when you ask for it.

## How you get one

Three things have to be true first:

1. You confirmed the Pattern was the same activity.
2. You said you would like help reducing it.
3. You pressed the button asking for ideas.

Until all three happen, nothing is requested and nothing is sent.

A short label you write yourself — something like *"saving images from the
browser"* — makes the ideas noticeably more useful, because it gives the
suggestion something human to work from.

## What you get back

Suggestions to weigh, not actions. Onceaway does not set anything up, install
anything, or run anything on your behalf. You read the idea and decide.

## They are not kept

Suggestions live for the current session only. Quit Onceaway and they are gone.
That is deliberate: they are generated text, not a record of your work, and
they are not treated as one.
