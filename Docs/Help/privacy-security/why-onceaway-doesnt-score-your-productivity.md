# Why Onceaway doesn't score your productivity

There is no productivity score in Onceaway, and there is not going to be one.

## What is not there

No focus score. No efficiency rating. No "time wasted" figure. No daily grade,
no streak, and no comparison against yesterday, against a target, or against
anyone else.

## Why not

Two reasons, and the first is simply honesty.

**Onceaway cannot measure it.** It sees which app was in front and that a file
of some type appeared in a folder. From that you cannot tell concentration from
confusion, or thinking time from wasted time. Any number built on it would be a
guess wearing a decimal point.

**It would change what the product is for.** The goal is to reduce repeated
work, not to judge how someone spends their day. The moment a tool starts
scoring you, you start performing for the score — and a tool you feel watched
by is not one you leave running.

## What you get instead

Description rather than judgement: what recurred, how often, across how many
days, and what is happening more than it used to. Facts you can act on, with
the interpretation left to the person who actually knows what the work was.
