# Why privacy comes before observation

One sentence governs how Onceaway is built:

**Privacy decides whether observation may happen. Observation never decides
privacy for itself.**

## What the sentence rules out

It rules out the arrangement most tools use, where everything is captured and
sensitive material is filtered out afterwards. That approach depends on the
filter being perfect, and it means the sensitive thing existed — even briefly —
in a place it should never have been.

In Onceaway, the question is asked first. Before an event is recorded, the
context decides whether it may be recorded in detail at all. If the answer is
no, the detail is never collected, so there is nothing to filter, redact or
leak.

## Why it is written this way round

Because the alternative is a promise you cannot keep. "We delete sensitive data
afterwards" depends on classification working every time, on nothing being
logged along the way, and on nothing being kept in a cache you forgot about.
"We never collect it" is a much smaller promise, and it is one that holds.

## What it means for you day to day

- Type a password and observation steps back before the keystrokes exist.
- Open your password manager and nothing is collected from inside it, with no
  option to change that.
- Move into a banking context and the detail stops.
- Add an app to Never Observe and it is respected, not merely tidied up later.
