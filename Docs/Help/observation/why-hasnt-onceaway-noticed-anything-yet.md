# Why hasn't Onceaway noticed anything yet?

Almost always one of four reasons. Work through them in order.

## 1. Observation is off

This is the most common one, because observation is **off** when you install
Onceaway and stays off until you start it.

Look at the ring in your menu bar. **Dashed** means off. Click it and choose
**Start Observation**. The foot of the sidebar says the same thing.

## 2. It has not happened enough times yet

Onceaway only reports something once it has seen the same structure **more than
once**. If you have been observing for an hour, there may genuinely be nothing
to say yet.

Give it a few working days. Repetition that spans several days is exactly what
it is looking for, and that takes several days to see.

## 3. The work is in a protected or excluded context

Detailed observation is suppressed in protected contexts, and nothing is
collected from inside apps on your **Never Observe** list. If the repeated work
you have in mind happens there, it will not appear.

Check **Settings → Privacy** for anything you excluded and may have forgotten.

## 4. The work is not visible to Onceaway yet

Onceaway sees app switches, file events in Downloads and Desktop, and — if you
enabled it — browser hostnames. Work that happens entirely inside one
application, with no file activity and no browser involvement, does not
currently produce enough signal to recognise.

That is a real limitation, not a setting. See
[When Onceaway gets it wrong](when-onceaway-gets-it-wrong.md).
