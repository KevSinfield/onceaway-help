# What is Onceaway?

Onceaway notices work you repeat on your Mac, helps you decide what is worth
reducing, and can assist with selected tasks when you choose.

That is the whole idea: **make repeated work go away**.

## What it actually does

Most people have a handful of small tasks they do again and again without ever
stopping to notice. Saving the same kind of file from the same place. Moving
between the same three apps to finish one job. Looking something up, copying it
somewhere, filing it.

Onceaway watches for that shape of repetition in the background, and when it
sees the same structure more than once it shows you what it found and asks a
simple question: *were these really the same task?*

You answer. Onceaway does not assume it knows.

## What it does not do

- It does not score you, rate you, or judge how you spend your day.
- It does not read what you type, what your files are called, or what is on
  your screen.
- It does not act on its own. Nothing happens to a file unless you approve the
  exact action first.
- It does not need AI to be useful. AI is optional and off until you add a key.

## Who it is for

People whose work has a lot of small, repeated administration in it: agency and
small-business owners, consultants, bookkeepers, recruiters, marketers and
other professional-services work. You need to be comfortable using a Mac. You
do not need to be technical.
