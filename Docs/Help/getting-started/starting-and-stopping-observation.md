# Starting and stopping observation

Observation is **off** when you first install Onceaway. You turn it on
yourself, and you can turn it off at any moment.

## The ring in your menu bar

Onceaway puts a small ring near your clock. It tells you the state at a glance:

| Ring | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashed** | Observation is off. Nothing is being recorded. |
| **Solid** | Observation is on. |

Click the ring to open the menu. It states the same thing in words —
*Observation: Off* or *Observation: On* — along with **Start Observation** or
**Pause Observation**.

The foot of the sidebar in the main window shows the same status.

## Turning it on

Click the ring, then **Start Observation**. That is all.

## Turning it off

Click the ring, then **Pause Observation**. Observation stops immediately.
Anything already recorded stays until your retention setting removes it, and
you can clear it yourself at any time.

## Quitting

Quitting Onceaway stops observation cleanly. Nothing runs when the app is not
running. Closing the main window is not the same as quitting — the app stays
available in the menu bar, and clicking **Open Onceaway** brings the window
back.
