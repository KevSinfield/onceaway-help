# First-run setup

The first time you open Onceaway it walks you through six short screens. It
takes about a minute, and it turns nothing on by itself.

## What the screens cover

1. **Welcome** — what Onceaway is for.
2. **Privacy first** — what is protected, and why privacy is decided before
   anything is observed.
3. **What Onceaway watches** — your three observation sources, showing their
   actual current settings.
4. **AI is optional** — what AI adds, and that everything works without it.
5. **How Onceaway works** — notice, confirm, assess, recommend, assist.
6. **You're ready** — where to look next.

## What finishing it does and does not do

Finishing setup records one thing: that you have seen it. It does **not**:

- turn on observation,
- turn on browser context,
- store an API key,
- change any privacy setting,
- send anything anywhere.

Anything optional stays optional.

## If you leave part-way

You can. Click any page in the sidebar and setup steps aside so you can look
around. A **Continue setup** link stays in the sidebar until you finish, and it
takes you back to the step you left. Nothing is locked and nothing is forced.

## Running it again

Open **Help** and choose **Run setup again**. This reopens the guide from the
beginning. It resets no setting, no review, no preference and no saved key.
