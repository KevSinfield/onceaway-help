# Why Onceaway keeps AI optional

Because a tool that stops being useful when a service is down, a key expires or
a bill goes unpaid is not really your tool.

## The principle

**Local Onceaway functionality never depends on an AI credential.**

Noticing repeated work, reviewing it, weighing it, tracking it over time and
performing the assisted move are all done on your Mac. None of them asks a
provider anything. Turn off your network, remove your key, never add one, and
the product still does the thing it exists to do.

## What AI is for, then

Two jobs that genuinely benefit from language, and only those:

- putting a Pattern into plain words,
- suggesting ways you might reduce it.

Both are conveniences layered on top of work already done locally. Neither is
load-bearing.

## Why this matters more than it sounds

If AI were required, three things would follow. Your private activity would
have to leave your Mac for the product to function at all. The product would
break in ways you cannot fix when a provider has an outage. And "privacy-first"
would become a slogan rather than a description, because the data would have to
go somewhere for anything to work.

Keeping AI optional is what lets the privacy promise stay a real one.
